# SALES USER SIDE B2B ACCESS

A B2B/DMC hotel booking portal for the travel trade. Partners (travel agents, tour operators, DMCs, corporates) search contracted UAE hotel rates, compare tariffs, and submit booking requests — all without needing an access code.

## Who it's for
- Travel agents, tour operators, DMCs and corporate travel desks
- Anyone in the travel trade looking for wholesale UAE hotel rates

## How it works
- **Open discovery**: Search, hotel profiles, View Rates, Tariff Calendar, Price Match, Offline Inquiry, Groups & MICE, Attractions, Transfers, Visa and Packages are all publicly accessible — no login or access code required.
- **Guest checkout**: Anyone can submit a booking request by providing a guest name and agency contact details (email, mobile, company, business type, country). No account needed.
- **Partner accounts**: Agencies can optionally create an account (email + password) to get their agency details prefilled at checkout and see all their bookings in one place via My Bookings. Partner sign-in is available from the header on every page.
- **Find a booking**: Guests without an account can look up a single booking by reference + agency email.
- **Operations flow**: All booking requests go to the UAE operations team for hotel confirmation. The portal never shows "Confirmed" — it shows "Requested" until operations updates the status.

## What it doesn't do
- No payment processing — bookings are requests, not purchases
- No cancellation or amendment by partners — those go through operations
- No tiered pricing (guest vs partner rates) — the access boundaries are shaped so tiers can be added later
- Admin, contracting, tariff and markup administration live in the separate Admin Extranet app
